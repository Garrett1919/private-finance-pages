Privacy Policy

Private Finance is a private, non-commercial application used solely by its operator to access and analyse the operator's own bank account information.

Data processed

The application may process account information made available through the connected open-banking provider, including account details, balances and transaction data.

Purpose

The data is processed only for the operator's personal financial overview and analysis.

Sharing

The application does not intentionally sell or disclose banking data to third parties. Data is only transmitted to service providers that are technically required to provide the requested banking connection or hosting functionality.

Storage

Banking data is handled only as necessary for the application's private use. Credentials for online banking are not stored by this website.

Access and revocation

Access to bank-account information is based on the operator's explicit authorization and can be revoked through the relevant banking or open-banking service where supported.

Contact

This application is operated privately by its owner and is not offered as a service to the public.
